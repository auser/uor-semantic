## Cross-corpus graph-accuracy probe

On 2026-08-01, the graph profile was evaluated against WikiText-2 raw train
and validation splits. The source mirror and split names are recorded at:

```text
https://cosmo.zip/pub/datasets/wikitext-2-raw/wiki.train.raw
https://cosmo.zip/pub/datasets/wikitext-2-raw/wiki.valid.raw
```

The downloaded source hashes were:

```text
train 6707892fa3788b5ab9ed78ab5ff37d9fe825f6011a2ad4fcd6a6d467f0e7da57
valid 4cd0f6876d07a413aa911261ff6d363c72d757d47f0fdd6015702014c89cb9c7
```

Selection was deterministic: retain non-empty lines of at least 80
characters, take every 140th eligible train line and every 14th eligible
validation line, and stop at 256 lines per split. This produced 111 and 119
prompt lines, which captured 888 construction and 952 held-out observations
with the pinned SmolLM2 revision, `max_context=8`, and `top_k=16`.

The default `r48-m16-k1` profile retained 100% graph coverage in both
directions. Overall graph top-K recall was 181 bps from train to validation
and 477 bps from validation to train. The symmetric reports were generated by
`scripts/cross_validate_graph_accuracy.sh`; raw downloaded and captured files
remain outside the repository, while these hashes and selection rules make the
measurement reproducible.

The same profile produced a 56,964-byte graph-only artifact for the train
direction and scanned 45,696 indexed regions across 952 held-out samples (48
per sample). The reverse artifact was also 56,964 bytes and scanned 42,624
regions across 888 samples. The portable fixture benchmark separately
reported, on an `aarch64` host with 31 samples and 10,000 iterations per
sample, 9 ns median route latency, 3 candidates, and 3 packed words per route.

## Scope of the evidence

The acceptance run verifies the Rust workspace, the deterministic fixture
pipeline, runtime allocation behavior, BDD traceability, and the release CLI.
It does not download a public model or execute a large external corpus during
CI. Live Hugging Face accuracy depends on the selected model revision, corpus,
capture settings, compiler configuration, and evaluation manifest.

The current capture bridge performs teacher-forced next-token observation over
a supplied corpus with a maximum 32-token suffix. Exact captured-context parity
and graph-only generalization are reported separately. Universal arbitrary-
prompt parity is not certified by this build.

`cargo deny` remains available through `just deny`; it is separate from the
normative `just vv` gate. The production runtime, compiler, and CLI have no
third-party Rust dependencies, while private conformance tooling uses pinned
`serde` and `toml` versions.
